# F05 · pure sale operation contract

Added version-1 draft, confirmation, collection, preparation, delivery, and
shift payloads plus a pure contract service. Classification is derived from
installment due days: zero-day cash, positive-day credit, or mixed. Approval is
a separate gate and confirmed orders are locked. Credit/mixed invoice on
confirmation; cash invoice on collection; FSC emits invoice+dispatch at
approval but delivery still requires full payment. Preparation does not impose
the collection gate. Ambiguous collection is a retryable conflict with no
fiscal authorization. Delivery consumes a typed domain-control result
(`SaleDeliveryGate`): credit approval, cash fully paid, or mixed due amounts
settled; this layer does not calculate money. FSC affects only contado.

Focal tests: 7 passing cases covering all classifications, approval/lock,
four fiscal paths and FSC, preparation/delivery, ambiguous collection, and
shift open/close. This remains a pure contract layer: transport/queue,
payment capture, fiscal adapters, turn ownership, and ERP/Odoo integration are
intentionally not implemented in F05. The pure ports now require one durable
`commitAndEnqueueIfAbsent` boundary, reconcile ambiguous collection before any
retry, and return typed shift conflicts; fakes test concurrent deduplication
and no-duplicate payment retry.
